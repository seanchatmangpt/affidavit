# Explanation: Operation Focus Doctrine

**Document Version:** `1.0`  
**Classification:** Pre-Emptive Rule 56 Civil Litigation Strategy  

---

## 1. Tactical Analogy: The 1967 Air Campaign

In June 1967, Operation Focus achieved strategic victory within three hours by neutralizing opposing runways before aircraft could take off.

In civil litigation, enterprise defense counsel seeks to launch attritional procedural bombers:
- Rule 12(b)(6) motions to dismiss for failure to state a claim.
- Extensive, expensive e-discovery requests to exhaust claimant resources.
- Disputing damages via competing forensic accounting experts.

The **Operation Focus Doctrine** uses cryptographic receipts to crater the procedural runways before the defense can file initial responsive pleadings.

---

## 2. The Five Concrete-Piercing Rule 56 Munitions

1. **Agency Ratification:** Automated telemetry commits corporate deployment directly, neutralizing Restatement (Third) of Agency § 2.03 defenses.
2. **Liquidated Restitution (§ 42):** Tariff cents are calculated at invocation time, providing liquidated damages that eliminate the need for speculative damage models.
3. **Constructive Trust:** Avoided hyperscaler costs are legally categorized as property held in constructive trust for the IP licensor.
4. **Mandatory Spoliation Inferences (FRCP 37(e)):** Any gap in nullifier sequences establishes bad-faith destruction of evidence, warranting default judgment.
5. **Privilege Stripping (Crime-Fraud Exception):** Attempting to counsel an enterprise to expunge or hide self-authenticating records pierces the attorney-client privilege.
