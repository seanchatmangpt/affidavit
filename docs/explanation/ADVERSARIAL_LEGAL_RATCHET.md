## 1. The Institutional Asymmetry

In corporate disputes involving high-throughput software systems, traditional defense counsel ("Matt Lopez" archetype) relies on narrative obfuscation, procedural delay, and evidentiary spoliation to evade commercial obligations. When a disruptive engine eliminates tens of millions of dollars in cloud spend, corporate reflexes prompt:
1. Denying use or asserting lack of employee authority (Restatement (Third) of Agency § 2.03 defenses).
2. Purging developer environments, deleting CI/CD logs, or wiping caches.
3. Asserting that telemetry outputs are unverified hearsay under FRE 803(6).

`affidavit` eliminates this defensive space by operating under the doctrine: **"Certify, Don't Decide — Make the Unverifiable Unconstructable."**

### Tooth 1: Self-Authenticating Records (FRE 902(14))
Under Federal Rule of Evidence 902(14), certified records generated by an electronic process or system that produces an accurate result are self-authenticating if supported by a certification. `affidavit` emits deterministic BLAKE3 rolling chains that require no live custodian testimony.

### Tooth 2: Structural Anti-Spoliation (FRCP 37(e))
FRCP 37(e) penalizes the failure to preserve electronically stored information with mandatory adverse inferences or default judgment. Every sealed CTRT registers a nullifier in `.affi/nullifiers/`. If an enterprise prunes or deletes logs, the nullifier sequence gap mathematically proves spoliation.

### Tooth 3: Liquidated Restitution (§ 42)
The Restatement (Third) of Restitution and Unjust Enrichment § 42 measures recovery by the value of the benefit obtained. `tariff_basis_cents` hardcodes the avoided hyperscaler compute spend into the signed token, eliminating the battle-of-the-experts over damages.

### Tooth 4: Individual Professional Responsibility (ABA Rules 1.2(d) & 3.4(a))
ABA Model Rule 1.2(d) forbids counsel from assisting a client in conduct known to be fraudulent. ABA Model Rule 3.4(a) prohibits unlawful obstruction of access to evidence. When counsel is confronted with an unbroken cryptographic chain, silence or denial violates professional ethics.
